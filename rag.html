5. What You Should Remember

Chunking

Large documents are broken into smaller pieces so relevant information can be retrieved.

Embedding

Text is converted into numbers called vectors. Similar meanings tend to have similar vectors.

Retrieval

The user's question is embedded and compared against the stored chunk embeddings.

Generation

The retrieved chunks and question are provided to the LLM, which generates the final response.

This is an educational simulation. No external APIs are used.
